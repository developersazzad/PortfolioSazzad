IP addresses recorded for security are stored in plain form in server logs where required for abuse handling, and in hashed form for download statistics, so individual readers cannot be identified from the analytics. I do not attempt to identify anonymous visitors.

03 Why I use your information

  • To answer you. Reading your enquiry and replying is the whole point of the contact form.
  • To prepare a proposal. If you ask about a project, understanding your requirement lets me quote scope, timeline and price.
  • To deliver agreed work. If we work together, your contact details and project information are needed to communicate and deliver.
  • To send updates you asked for. If you subscribed, you receive occasional notes about new work, tools or availability — and nothing else.
  • To keep the site working and safe. Rate limiting, spam filtering, and security monitoring to stop the forms being abused.
  • To understand what's useful. Aggregate statistics about which pages and projects visitors spend time on.

I do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling, and I do not use it to send you marketing you did not ask for.

07 Who I share information with

Nobody else — and never for money. I do not sell, rent, trade or publish your personal information, and I do not add you to any list you didn't ask for.

Information is shared only with:

  • Service providers listed above, strictly as needed for the site and email to function.
  • Professional advisers (accountant, legal) if required for invoicing or a dispute — under confidentiality.
  • Authorities, only where the law compels it and a lawful request is made.

The only exception to confidentiality about projects is portfolio rights: I may mention a project I delivered, in general terms, unless we agreed an NDA. Client data inside that project — users, orders, customer records — is never published or reused.

08 How long information is kept

  • Contact enquiries: up to 24 months from the last reply, so a returning conversation still makes sense.
  • Project clients: contact and project records are kept for the duration of the engagement plus up to 24 months afterwards for support and accounting.
  • Subscribers: until you unsubscribe. A minimal record (email + unsubscribed status) is then kept for up to 6 months so I don't accidentally email you again.
  • CV download statistics: up to 12 months, in hashed/anonymised form.
  • Server logs: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Spam and abuse records: up to 12 months from the block or report.

When the period ends, records are deleted or irreversibly anonymised. If you ask me to delete something earlier, I will — see section 11.

09 How the information is protected

  • The site is served over HTTPS with an enforced secure connection (HSTS), so traffic is encrypted in transit.
  • Form submissions are checked for abuse with spam filtering and rate limiting, and validated before storage.
  • The admin area is protected by authentication, session controls and access checks; only I can read enquiries.
  • Download statistics are hashed, so individual visitors are not identifiable from them.
  • Backups are restricted and access to the hosting, database and email accounts uses strong, unique credentials.
  • Software and dependencies are kept updated to reduce known vulnerability exposure.

No system is perfectly secure, and I won't pretend otherwise. What I can commit to is reasonable, proportionate protection, minimal collection in the first place, and telling you promptly if a breach affecting your information ever occurs.

10 If you're a client: your project data

When I build or maintain a system for you, that system's data — users, customers, orders, content — belongs to you and is your responsibility as its owner. My commitments are:

  • Access is limited to me and anyone you explicitly authorise, and only for the work requested.
  • I don't copy production data into personal environments; where testing needs realistic data, it's anonymised.
  • Credentials and API keys are handled through a password manager or a protocol you approve, and rotated on handover.
  • Data is not reused for other clients, published, or used to train anything.
  • On completion or on request, access is removed and local copies deleted after an agreed retention window.
  • An NDA can be signed before any confidential material is shared — just ask.
