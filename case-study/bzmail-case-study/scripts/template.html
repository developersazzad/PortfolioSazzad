Section 02 · The why

Why BZmail exists

One Gmail account can’t run real email marketing. BZmail was designed as three connected surfaces — engine, admin room and user panel — so owners, teams and customers each get exactly what they need.

WHY THE ENGINE

Gmail’s hard sending caps

THE PROBLEMGmail caps every free account at ~500 emails/day, then blocks it. A single sender can never scale a real campaign.
THE BZMAIL FIXA rotating pool of Gmail SMTP servers (smtp.gmail.com : 587) with per-server Active toggles and instant failover — volume now has no ceiling.
↓
WHY THE ADMIN PANEL

Owners were flying blind

THE PROBLEMSpreadsheets, scattered scripts and separate tools — zero visibility over users, servers, campaigns and results.
THE BZMAIL FIXOne admin control room: users, guests, servers, projects, campaigns, templates, live analytics and Copy / Excel / PDF exports.
↓
WHY THE USER PANEL (PWA)

Customers on any device

THE PROBLEMEnd-users needed access from any phone with no app-store installs and no clunky legacy interfaces.
THE BZMAIL FIXAn installable PWA with guest login & registration and a high-UX, iconic dark-glass interface that feels native everywhere.
↓
WHY THE IMPACT

Sending blind burns domains

THE PROBLEMNo opens, no clicks, no bounce data — lists decay, reputations burn, money disappears.
THE BZMAIL FIXReal tracking on everything: open / click / bounce rates, fail-lists and don’t-try lists keep campaigns measurable and domains safe.

Section 03 · The product

One product, three surfaces

Every screen below is real product UI — explore the public website, the admin control room and the PWA user panel.

SURFACE A

Public website — the storefront

A dark, conversion-focused landing experience that sells the promise in seconds: personalization, no-limit sending and a full marketing funnel.

  • Hero + funnel storytelling with pricing tiers for every business size
  • Entry points for Sign up, Contact and instant Send Mail
  • Testimonials, feature deep-dives and a fully responsive layout
https://bzmail.uk
SURFACE B

Admin panel — the control room

Everything an operator needs, in one place. Tap the tabs on the laptop to walk through the real modules.

  • Live KPI cards: open rate, click rate, bounce rate, campaigns
  • SMTP server cards with Active failover toggles & guest servers
  • Users, guests, projects & campaigns with Copy / Excel / PDF export
  • Theme engine: light / dark / gradient menus & headers, LTR + RTL
SURFACE C

User panel — installable PWA

A modern, iconic mobile experience. Guests sign in or register in seconds and the app installs straight from the browser — no app store needed.

  • Guest login + one-tap registration flows
  • High-UX dark-glass UI with gradient stage and tactile inputs
  • Installable, offline-friendly, fast on any device
