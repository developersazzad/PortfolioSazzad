Software Details

Inside the SystemEasy Codebase

A production tour of the seven modules — exactly what ships in the live build at se.wedevspro.com.

Live Demo Case study — problem & solution
POS

High-Speed Interactive POS Terminal

  • Keyboard-shortcut driven checkout for the fastest possible counter experience.
  • Split payment in one bill: cash + card + bKash/mobile banking combined.
  • Offline-ready cash register: shift opening balance and closing handover report.
  • Barcode scanner support with 80mm/58mm thermal printer auto receipt cutting.
INV

Multi-Location Inventory & Warehousing

  • Product types: single, variable (size/color templates), combo bundles and modifiers.
  • Warehouse rack / row / shelf location tracking (product_racks).
  • Batch number and expiry-date management — essential for pharmacy, food and cosmetics.
  • Automatic low-stock alerts and stock adjustment with damage/scrap logging.
ACC

True Double-Entry Accounting & Ledger

  • Fully automatic chart of accounts (accounts, account_types, account_transactions).
  • Every sale or payment posts debit and credit in the background, instantly.
  • Balance sheet, trial balance, profit & loss, cash flow statement.
  • Customer and supplier ledgers with due tracking.
PUR

Intelligent Purchase & Supply Chain

  • Purchase order generation, goods receipt notes and purchase returns.
  • Supplier due tracking with partial payment scheduling.
  • FIFO batch matching: each sale maps to the oldest batch for exact gross profit and margin.
RES

Restaurant & Hospitality Suite

  • Restaurant table layout and seating management (res_tables).
  • Kitchen order tickets (KOT): counter sends digital orders straight to the kitchen.
  • Separate service-charge handling for dine-in, takeaway and home delivery.
ACL

Enterprise RBAC & Audit Log

  • Spatie Laravel-Permission integration with 81+ granular permissions.
  • Custom roles for manager, cashier, stock keeper and accountant.
  • Full audit trail (activity_log): who edited or deleted which invoice, and when.
CUR

Multi-Currency, Multi-Tax & Notification Gateway

  • 141 global currencies and multiple tax groups (VAT / GST / flat tax).
  • SMS notification engine (Twilio, Nexmo) with automatic invoice-link updates to customers.
  • Secure public invoice viewer (/invoice/{token}) and payment receipt sharing.
Senior Engineering

20-Year-Class Engineering Highlights

The advanced techniques applied inside SystemEasy that separate it from off-the-shelf POS scripts.

LOCK

Pessimistic Row Locking

lockForUpdate() inside DB transactions makes double-selling the same stock unit physically impossible.

MATH

BCMath Money Precision

Zero floating-point discrepancy on currency and tax math — down to the last paisa.

DATA

Server-Side DataTables

Pagination, filtering and search at database level: rendering under 100ms even with millions of rows.

FPM

Isolated PHP 7.4-FPM + Nginx

Dedicated FastCGI socket with OPcache tuning on Ubuntu 24.04 LTS: downtime-free deploys beside PHP 8.3 projects.

SAFE

Installer Vulnerability Patching

Deep security audit locked the installer routes on the live system so no unwanted reset can ever happen.

FIFO

FIFO Batch Matching

Every sale auto-maps to the oldest purchase batch, producing exact COGS, gross profit and margin.
