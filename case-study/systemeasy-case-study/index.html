Case Study · Section 01

Why SystemEasy Had to Exist

Fast-growing retail chains outgrow generic POS software. Five costly failures showed up again and again in the field — and SystemEasy was engineered to remove every one of them.

01
SYNC

Multi-Outlet Stock Sync Leaks

One central warehouse feeding many branches: desynced stock readings caused over-selling and silent inventory loss across outlets.

02
LEDG

No Double-Entry Accounting

Most POS tools log only sales and expenses. Without double-entry bookkeeping (Asset = Liability + Equity), month-end audits and balance sheets never matched.

03
RACE

Checkout Race Conditions

Several cashiers selling the last unit in the same millisecond created database lock conflicts, double selling and corrupted stock counts.

04
FIFO

FIFO Costing Complexity

Goods bought at different purchase prices over time made true COGS and gross profit per product impossible to calculate.

05
KOT

Fragmented Restaurant Workflow

Table booking, kitchen order tickets (KOT), service staff tracking and modifiers never integrated cleanly with a retail POS.

Case Study · Section 02

The Solution & The Impact

A modular, domain-driven monolith on Laravel — every domain engine below was built to kill one of the five failures above, and the metrics prove it.

Engineering Decisions That Solved It

LOCK

Pessimistic Row Locking

lockForUpdate() inside DB transactions makes double-selling the same stock unit physically impossible.

MATH

BCMath Money Precision

Zero floating-point discrepancy on currency and tax math — down to the last paisa.

DATA

Server-Side DataTables

Pagination, filtering and search at database level: rendering under 100ms even with millions of rows.

FIFO

FIFO Batch Matching

Every sale auto-maps to the oldest purchase batch, producing exact COGS, gross profit and margin.

BOOK

Automatic Double-Entry Posting

Each sale or payment posts debit + credit to the chart of accounts in the background — books always audit-ready.

FPM

Isolated PHP 7.4-FPM + Nginx

Dedicated FastCGI socket with OPcache tuning on Ubuntu 24.04 LTS: downtime-free deploys beside PHP 8.3 projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SystemEasy?
SystemEasy is a free, open POS and inventory management software — a complete shop, business and inventory management system with double-entry accounting, built on Laravel 5.8, PHP 7.4 and MySQL 8.0.
Who developed SystemEasy?
SystemEasy was developed by Sazzad Hossain (@developersazzad), a full-stack web developer based in Chattogram, Bangladesh, as principal Laravel architect and lead engineer.
Is SystemEasy free to use?
Yes. SystemEasy is positioned as a free POS management system. A live demo is available at se.wedevspro.com with demo login admin / admin123.
Which technologies power SystemEasy?
Laravel 5.8, PHP 7.4-FPM, MySQL 8.0 (InnoDB), AdminLTE 2.4, Blade, jQuery, server-side DataTables, Pusher WebSockets, mPDF/DomPDF, Nginx, Redis and Cloudflare WAF/SSL.
Does SystemEasy support multiple outlets, currencies and restaurants?
Yes — multi-location inventory with stock transfers, 141 global currencies, multiple tax groups, and a full restaurant suite with tables, kitchen order tickets and delivery service charges.
Where can I watch SystemEasy video tutorials?
On the official YouTube playlist of Sazzad Hossain (@sazzadhossain01), linked from the Tutorials page of this case study.
