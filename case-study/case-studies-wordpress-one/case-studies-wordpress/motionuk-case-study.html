02 Why this site · What was built

The challenge & the custom build

MotionUK recruits students from South Asia and Africa for universities in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and Europe. The agency needed a website that could explain the process, earn trust, and collect enquiries that counsellors could act on.

Problems to solve

  1. 01
    Unqualified enquiries

    Students contacted the agency by phone and WhatsApp without key details, so counsellors had to ask for the basics (qualification, IELTS score, budget) again and again.

  2. 02
    A complex journey

    7 destinations, dozens of courses, IELTS and no-IELTS routes, visas and accommodation were hard to explain in one conversation.

  3. 03
    A trust gap

    International students need proof before sharing documents: real partners, real student outcomes and a real UK office.

  4. 04
    A mobile-first audience

    Most visitors browse on phones, and wide desktop banners were cropped until they were unreadable.

Special features & customisation

01

Smart inquiry funnel

A 9-field qualifying form with 10 qualification types, 12 English-proficiency options and budget/fund bands. Every lead arrives ready to assess.

WPForms · Custom logic
02

Journey infographic

The “DRAGON movement” roadmap turns a confusing admissions process into 6 clear milestones: Decision, Ready, Admission, Go, Operation, Nationality.

Custom design · Elementor
03

Art-directed sliders

Separate slide sets for desktop (1200×500) and mobile (450×650) across 7 destinations, so no headline gets cropped on a phone.

Responsive art direction
04

Trust layer

A partner-country news ticker, a 43-logo university carousel and a 10-student testimonial slider with names, degrees and universities.

Element Pack · EA
05

App-like mobile UX

A sticky bottom navigation bar (Home, Services, Notice, Contact), an off-canvas menu and live search. It works like a native app.
