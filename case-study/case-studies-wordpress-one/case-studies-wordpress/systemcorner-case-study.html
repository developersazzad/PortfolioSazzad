02 Why this site · What was built

The challenge & the custom build

System Corner is a London-registered agency that sells web design, software, LMS, SEO and marketing to clients in the UK and USA. It had a large portfolio, but no way to show it properly and no way to sell online.

Problems to solve

  1. 01
    Manual, quote-only sales

    Every service was sold through chat and custom quotes. There were no public prices, no checkout and a slow sales cycle.

  2. 02
    A hidden portfolio

    Hundreds of delivered projects weren't organised, so prospects couldn't find relevant work in their category.

  3. 03
    High-ticket friction

    Packages worth $399–$749+ needed flexible payment and a pre-sale consultation step before clients would commit.

  4. 04
    Heavy media

    Long full-page portfolio screenshots made pages heavy and slow.
