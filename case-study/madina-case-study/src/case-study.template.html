01 Why this site

A real shop with a mixed catalogue — and customers living on their phones.

Madina Clothing runs a physical store in the UK and sells across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. They needed one place where a shopper could find a product, check the size, ask a question and pay — on mobile, without sending a single DM.

Problem 01

Selling through DMs

Prices, stock and sizes were answered one chat at a time. That doesn't scale, and orders were lost at night and weekends.

A self-serve shop with PayPal checkout, open 24/7
Problem 02

A very mixed catalogue

Abayas in S-52 / M-54 / L-56, thobes, kufis, perfumes in ml, bakhoor burners, Qurans, prayer mats — each type needs different data.

16 nested categories plus variable products
Problem 03

Size doubt = returns

Modest-wear buyers worry about length and fit. Without a size guide and a quick way to ask, they leave or return.

Size chart popup + WhatsApp & phone, one tap away
Problem 04

Shoppers start on video

Customers discover products on reels, but a normal product page doesn't feel like the video they just watched.

A custom Shoppable Reel plugin, built from scratch

02 Solution & impact

A customised Woodmart build, plus a plugin made only for this shop.

★ Custom plugin · v1.5.0

Shoppable Reel by DevSazzad

I wrote this WordPress plugin from scratch (PHP + vanilla JS) so the store can sell directly from vertical video, the way its customers already shop.

  • Reels carousel, each reel linked to a WooCommerce product with its thumbnail, live price, discount badge and a "Go to product" button.
  • Fullscreen modal player with prev/next, keyboard controls, a sound toggle, share and a side playlist.
  • Floating mini-reel pinned to the product gallery. You can drag it with touch or mouse, expand it or minimise it.
  • Built to be reliable: it uses the YouTube IFrame API so mute and autoplay behave consistently, and a MutationObserver so it also works inside the Elementor editor.
// sr-frontend.js — dependable playback loadYouTubeAPI().then(YT => new YT.Player(id, { playerVars:{ autoplay, mute: muted ? 1 : 0 }, events:{ onReady: e => e.target.playVideo() } }));

Category architecture

16 categories in a nested mega-menu: Men's › Thobe · Kufi · Moroccan Thobes, and Lifestyle › Quran · Bakhoor Burners · Frames · Books.

Size guide & variations

Variable products with S-52 / M-54 / L-56 lengths and a size-chart popup on every garment, so buyers ask fewer questions and return less.

App-like mobile UX

A sticky bottom toolbar (Home · Shop · Cart · Account · Menu), off-canvas filters and a two-column product grid that's easy to use with your thumb.

Trust & contact

A floating WhatsApp + phone widget, a trust block (UK store, premium quality, UK support, secure delivery) and social links in the header.

AJAX shop experience

Price filter, show 9/12/18/24 products, grid switcher, sorting, quick view, wishlist and compare. None of it reloads the page.

Checkout & ownership

WooCommerce PayPal Payments for secure checkout. A Woodmart child theme and Elementor let the owner update banners and pages himself.
