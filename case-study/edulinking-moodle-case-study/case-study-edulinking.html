01

The problem — a strong offer with no front door

  • Reputation, no pipeline. Internationally accredited courses, a 6-month certificate and funded publication were selling through founder DMs, PDFs and forwarded posts.
  • Three products, one audience. Free masterclass, paid certificate with 3-month internship, and research publication each needed a page, a fee and a deadline — without splitting the brand.
  • Payments had to feel local. BDT pricing, bKash, bank transfer and card, with a free seat-booking step before any money moves.
  • Four linked identities. EduLinking, BCET UK degrees, the NCC student network and the CARSIT DBA had to read as one pathway, not four websites.
02

The outcome — book first, pay after, self-managed

  • Elementor page system on a Woodmart child theme — hero with two paths, founder authority block, program overview, curriculum accordion, testimonials, batch schedule and fee. The client edits every one of them alone.
  • One booking funnel: free seat hold → emailed payment instructions → post-payment form. WPForms and Contact Form 7 replace inbox chaos with a real lead list.
  • Friction-free pricing: ৳20,000 standard, 40% early-bird at ৳12,000, bKash / bank / card, no USD transfer for Bangladeshi applicants.
  • Mobile-first by default — persistent bottom nav (Home · Ongoing · Notice · Contact), sticky notice board, cross-links into BCET degree and CARSIT DBA paths. Homepage load event ~1.1 s.
