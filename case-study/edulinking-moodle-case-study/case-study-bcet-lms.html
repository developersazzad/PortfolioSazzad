Case study · 01 — Education platform

Brit College of Engineering & Technology LMS

A Moodle platform rebuilt as the college's daily learning home — 21 UK award-bearing courses, 10 UK/EU professors and a real accessibility layer, instead of scattered PDFs and WhatsApp links.

01

The problem — a British degree running on file dumps

  • Delivery was scattered. Slides, notices and assignments travelled through chat groups and drive links, so nothing was trackable for students or lecturers.
  • 21 concurrent modules, three time zones. Degree, diploma and professional tracks (Data Science, Cyber Security, Web & Python, Research) each needed their own structure, deadline rhythm and lecturer.
  • Compliance, not decoration. Special-education and international students required WCAG-style reading, contrast and motion controls — not a one-line checkbox.
  • Zero admin headcount. Enrolment, publishing and notices had to run inside Moodle, without a ticket to a developer.
02

The outcome — one login for the whole programme

  • Custom branded front page over the mb2nl Moodle theme: typed hero, Degree and Professional course grids, 10-profile faculty rail, and every call-to-action wired to the UK admissions portal.
  • Self-enrolment on every module — 50 seats on degree courses, instant join, no manual step for the office.
  • Accessibility toolbar shipped: readable font, text size and spacing, six contrast modes, reading guide and mask, stop animations — settings persist per student.
  • Light and responsive: catalogue page interactive at ~0.8 s on LiteSpeed, 320 px to 1920 px, dark logo variant and a mobile mega menu.
